# Phase 12 Forensics

## Experiments and evidence

- Phase 7 isolated optional-baseline variants; scores were close and did not
  establish a specification winner.
- Phase 9 inventoried 728 source-identity series; exact, weekly, biweekly,
  monthly, month-end and explicit-only alternatives remain indistinguishable
  as official rules.
- Phase 10 showed 23/25 first diverge at safe amount; downstream ranking and
  validation are not the primary defect.
- Phase 11 confirmed 54 tests, 250 output rows and deterministic reruns.

## Counterfactuals

The machine-readable `PHASE_7_BOUNDARY_FORENSICS.json` and
`PHASE_9_RECURRENCE_FORENSICS.json` contain the full policy/model tables.
They are diagnostic evidence only; counterfactual score is not specification
proof.

## First divergence

The earliest confirmed divergence remains the forecast/safe-amount boundary,
propagating to earliest date and then candidate/status/method/plan fields.

## Conclusion

No evidence justifies another broad production semantic rewrite. The safest
deterministic contract is explicit confirmed movements plus stable fixed
recurrence; all other unresolved boundaries remain configurable and documented.
